# J.O.A.T. Personality Controls

Commands:
- `tone` — show current tone
- `set tone friendly`
- `set tone nice`
- `set tone sarcastic`
- `set tone moody`
- `set tone playful`
- `set tone professional`
- `set tone calm`
- `set tone energetic`
- `set tone concise`
- `set emotion 70` — bounded emotional expression from 0–100
- `set mode study`

Personality changes style only. It does not change factual safety rules or tool permissions.
